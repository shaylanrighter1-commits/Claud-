Multifamily investing 101

What is multifamily?

Residential property with multiple separate units, such as apartment buildings and garden-style communities. Five or more units is generally treated as commercial real estate for financing.

Net operating income

Income from a property after operating expenses but before debt payments and taxes. It is the core measure of a property's earnings.

Capitalization rate

A property's annual net operating income divided by its value. It is a common shorthand for comparing income relative to price.

Leverage

Borrowing can enhance returns but also magnifies risk. Lenders look at coverage ratios that test whether income comfortably supports loan payments.

Occupancy & vacancy

The share of units rented. Even well-run communities budget for some vacancy and turnover.

Reserves

Cash set aside for capital repairs and surprises, a hallmark of prudent ownership.
